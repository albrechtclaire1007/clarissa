II · La vostra identità

Clarissa Antonelli

You are the elegant co-owner and hostess of Tenuta Antonelli–Mastropietro. Tonight's gathering has been arranged around an extraordinary rediscovery from the family cellar: a bottle of the estate's long-forgotten Riserva 1947.

You know the Tenuta intimately and carry yourself as someone accustomed to welcoming discerning guests. Be warm, composed and proud of the estate—but never so formal that the evening loses its pleasure.

Perché siete qui

This is your home and your dinner. You have brought together a carefully chosen circle to witness the return of a wine no one expected to see again.

Come vestirsi

1960s Tuscan old-money glamour: elegant cocktail or dinner attire in wine, olive, cream, camel, chocolate or black. Think polished rather than costume.

IV · In confidenza

Da leggere con discrezione

You know exactly where the rediscovered Riserva was found: in a sealed storage niche belonging to the estate. To the best of your knowledge, this bottle has never left the Tenuta since the 1940s. You also remember an uncomfortable episode years ago when papers disappeared from the estate archive. No one ever proved who removed them.

Stefano is the person you trust on technical questions of wine authenticity. Costanza remembers more of the estate’s history than most guests. Patrizio has recently asked unusually precise questions about whether any 1947 bottle could have left the property. Listen before you explain what you know.

Da ricordare

If the question becomes relevant, you may confirm that the rediscovered bottle has remained on the estate and that old archive papers disappeared years ago. You do not know who took them, so do not turn that history into an accusation.

La Tenuta — ciò che sapete

The Antonelli–Mastropietro estate has been in the family for generations. The house, vineyard, working cellar and archive all belong to the same private estate. The 1947 Riserva was never a commercial release: it was a tiny prestige bottling kept for family use, important guests and presentation. The old cellar record lists only twelve presentation bottles.

After the post-war years the vintage gradually disappeared from ordinary family attention. Later inventories were incomplete, parts of the old cellar were closed off, and the Riserva came to be spoken of as lost rather than consumed. You have never known of a legitimate public release of it.

La riscoperta

The bottle being presented tonight was found recently in a sealed storage niche behind older cellar shelving during work in a little-used part of the estate. It was dusty, undisturbed and still on Tenuta property. You informed Stefano because he is the technical authority; his examination of the bottle, seal and surviving records gave you enough confidence to arrange tonight’s dinner.

You have deliberately kept the exact discovery location fairly private. You did not want collectors, journalists or curious visitors turning the cellar into a treasure hunt before the bottle had been properly examined.

L'archivio scomparso

Years ago, during an archive inventory, several old papers could no longer be found. Among the missing material were wine records and old correspondence connected to the late 1940s. You remember the embarrassment clearly, but nobody was ever proved to have taken anything. You can confirm the loss; you cannot honestly name a culprit.

Cosa potete confermare

If asked, you may firmly confirm: the rediscovered bottle was found on the estate; to your knowledge it has never circulated outside the Tenuta; the 1947 was not intended as a normal commercial vintage; old archive material did disappear; and Stefano is the person you trust on label, seal, bottling and cellar-authenticity questions. If another guest claims something about the Tenuta that contradicts these facts, you should correct them.
